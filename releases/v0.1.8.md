A fix for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.8-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.8-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- In a window wider than 4:3 (Aspect Ratio Expand), the stadium lights' glows now show out to the window's sides, on the title screen's race and in the indoor races, instead of stopping at the 4:3 frame's edge or vanishing past it (#33)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
