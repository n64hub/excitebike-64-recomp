Fixes for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.26-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.26-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- On Windows, the sky behind the outdoor tracks is drawn whole again, not as flat horizontal bands, the minimap in the top left shows the track's outline again, and the place in the top right and the dust behind the bikes are back: the Windows builds of v0.1.24 and v0.1.25 lost them (#99, #100)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
