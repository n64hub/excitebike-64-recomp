Fixes for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.25-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.25-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- With Draw Distance on Far, the rocks and canyon walls ahead on the outdoor tracks no longer pop in at a straight edge over a crest, as they did with Original too: they are drawn nearly three times as far and fade into the fog as everything nearer does (#63)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
