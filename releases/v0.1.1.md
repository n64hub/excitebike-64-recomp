A fix for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.1-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.1-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by v0.1.0 is kept, and so are the season and the settings.

- Widescreen: the bikes' dust, mud, splashes and exhaust puffs are drawn out to the window's edges, where they used to vanish at the old 4:3 frame's sides

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
