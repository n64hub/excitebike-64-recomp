Two fixes for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.3-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.3-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- The black before the title no longer flashes a strip of noise across the top of the screen. The game copies a track's models into the picture it isn't showing while a race loads, and the race's first frame clears that picture black; the renderer showed the copy over the black for a frame
- Bikes cast their shadows on the ground again, in races and the attract demo (#6)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
