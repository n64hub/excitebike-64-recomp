Fixes for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.21-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.21-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- Fixed the evenly spaced green and black vertical lines across Original Excitebike, the NES game under Special Tracks, in a wide window (Aspect Ratio Expand) at a resolution above the game's own: its title, track select, crowd, track and HUD draw whole again (#62)
- The Desert's campfire smoke rises as one column at a display rate above the game's, where a puff or two jumped up or down it (#58)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
