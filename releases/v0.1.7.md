A fix for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.7-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.7-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- The translucent checkered flag that covers the screen after you win a race now fills the whole picture, top to bottom and, in a window wider than 4:3, out to the window's sides, its squares the console's size and shape; while it shows, the race's times, map and gauges stay at the window's sides instead of jumping into the 4:3 frame (#30)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
