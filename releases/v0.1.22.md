Fixes for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.22-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.22-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- The place arrow over the rider ahead follows it on the frames between the game's own at a display rate above the game's (120 Hz, say), where it often held still for a few frames and then jumped. It can still do that for a moment as it sweeps fast across the picture (#65)
- Rider Select's stats labels (Landing, Cornering, Jumping, Turbo) stay whole as the card slides out and in at a display rate above the game's, where letters dropped out in the frames between: the fix came in v0.1.21, and this release plays it again (#78)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
