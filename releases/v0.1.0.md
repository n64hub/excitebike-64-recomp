The first release of Excitebike 64: Recompiled for Windows and Linux: a native PC port of the N64 game made by static recompilation. It is not an emulator and contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (a big-endian `.z64`, 16 MiB, SHA-1 `c2fac422af135409c1b38569b309f9d452c81c35`). Other regions and revisions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**What works:** the splashes, the title and the menus; Season's races and the rest of its tracks; Exhibition races of up to four players in a split screen, each gamepad playing a player of its own with its Rumble Pak, a gamepad pulled out and plugged in again going back to its player; the pause menu. The season and the settings are saved to the game's EEPROM and kept across a restart. Sound (the game's own audio code, recompiled), and keyboard and gamepad input.

**Enhancements**, each an option on the settings menu's Graphics tab with an Original value that plays as the console does:

- High resolution at the window's size, with anti-aliasing
- Smooth motion at the display's refresh rate, blended between the game's frames
- Widescreen: in a window wider than 4:3, one player's race and two players' split one above the other reach the window's edges, with the HUD at its sides; the other split screens and the menus stay in the middle 4:3, the menus' backdrop reaching the edges
- Keyboard and gamepad controls, rebindable

**Not checked yet:** the track editor and Custom Tracks, time trials' ghosts (the game keeps them on a Controller Pak, which the runtime says isn't there), long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/excitebike-64](https://n64hub.com/excitebike-64/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp#readme) has how to build it yourself.
