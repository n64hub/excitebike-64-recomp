A new option for Excitebike 64: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux. You still need your own ROM of the USA release, Rev 1 (the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0) have the rest: what works and how to run it).

**Windows:** unzip `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.6-windows-x64.zip` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled.exe` from that folder. **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Excitebike64Recompiled-v0.1.6-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Excitebike64Recompiled` in that folder. A ROM stored by an earlier release is kept, and so are the season, the settings and the Controller Pak.

- On the stadium tracks, the lights' glow no longer shows through the hills in front of them, and when the lights come into view their lens flare of coloured hexagons is drawn, as on the console: the game judges both from the frame's depth, which the recomp now copies back for it (#23)

Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/excitebike-64-recomp/issues).
