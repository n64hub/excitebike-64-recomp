# Excitebike 64's frame-dump plan: one run of the desktop build through every screen a player meets, played in a 16:9,
# a 4:3 and a 32:9 window while the build dumps its frames, the three dumps then checked against each other by machine.
# A check fails only what a plan plays, so a scene a player reports a fault in that this doesn't reach is added here by
# the work that fixes it.
# Each line is a step at seconds since the program started: `key <name>` presses and releases a key in the window,
# `down <name>` and `up <name>` hold and release one, `shot <name>` captures the window, `poke <addr> <value>` writes a
# 32-bit word of RDRAM, and `until <addr> <mask> <value> <timeout s> [key]` waits until the word at addr anded with
# mask is value, pressing key every 1.5 s meanwhile if it isn't yet; the later steps move on by its wait, so the three
# runs keep step with the game, not the clock, and an until unmet by its timeout fails the run. Addresses are the USA
# Rev 1 ROM's (syms/). Keys are the keyboard's defaults: Enter Start, Space A, Shift B, the arrows the stick.
# The screen shown is the word at 0x800F5164, a Screen of the table at 0x800CDF7C, 0x2C bytes each: the main menu
# 0x800CE0DC, Options 0x800CE058, Special Tracks 0x800CE0B0, Rider Select 0x800CE108, the title 0x800CE1E4. The game
# state is the word at 0x800C3308: 0x800C32B4 a race, 0x800C3298 the menus. gGame is at 0x800F0710: its main menu
# cursor at +4 (0x800F0714, the modes from 0 in the menu's order), Options' cursor in the high half of +0x448
# (0x800F0B58). A menu fades in under its screen word and drops a key in the fade, so each step whose miss would leave
# the plan out of step presses until RAM shows it took.
# Two steps go past what keys can do in a few seconds. A race's end: the computer drives the player's bike home over
# one lap, the word at 0x800E4848 written as 0x01000000 (gRiders[0].isCpu, which the game sets itself as a player
# finishes, and the three bytes after it cleared) and the event's laps, the high half of 0x800F0F68, as 1 beside its 6
# riders, both once the race is under way (the event's state at 0x800F0F48 is 5), since its start sets the laps; the
# finish then waits for A. And the credits, left by Start after 17 s.
